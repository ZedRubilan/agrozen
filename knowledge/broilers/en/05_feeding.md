# Broiler Feeding Phases: Starter, Grower, and Finisher

Modern broiler genetics enable a chick to grow from 42 grams to 2.5 kilograms within just 6 weeks. However, achieving this potential requires balanced, commercial crumbled and pelleted rations. Feeding homemade ground grain mixtures or boiled eggs results in severe amino acid deficiencies, rickets, and 50% stunted body weight.

---

## 1. The Three Primary Feeding Phases

| Parameter | Phase 1: Starter | Phase 2: Grower | Phase 3: Finisher |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | **Days 1 – 10 (or 14)** | **Days 11 (or 15) – 28** | **Days 29 – 42** |
| **Feed Form** | Fine Crumb (1.5–2 mm) | Pellets (2.5–3 mm) | Pellets (3–3.5 mm) |
| **Crude Protein** | **21 – 23%** | **19 – 20%** | **17.5 – 18.5%** |
| **Metabolizable Energy** | 3000 – 3050 kcal/kg | 3100 – 3150 kcal/kg | 3200 – 3250 kcal/kg |
| **Primary Goal** | Organ, skeletal, digestive, and immune development | Rapid muscle and skeletal frame accretion | Breast and thigh meat deposition, market weight |

---

## 2. The 3-Day Feed Transition Protocol
A broiler's digestive tract is acutely sensitive to abrupt ration shifts. Sudden changes cause dysbiosis, wet droppings, and feed refusal.
* Always transition over **3 consecutive days**:
  * **Day 1 of transition:** 70% current feed + 30% new feed;
  * **Day 2 of transition:** 50% current feed + 50% new feed;
  * **Day 3 of transition:** 25% current feed + 75% new feed;
  * **Day 4:** 100% new feed.

---

## 3. Daily Feed Intake per Bird Benchmark

| Age (Week) | Daily Feed per Bird (g) | Cumulative Intake (g) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1 (Days 1–7)** | 15 – 25 g | ~130 – 160 g |
| **Week 2 (Days 8–14)** | 35 – 55 g | ~450 – 500 g |
| **Week 3 (Days 15–21)** | 65 – 85 g | ~1000 – 1100 g |
| **Week 4 (Days 22–28)** | 95 – 120 g | ~1800 – 1900 g |
| **Week 5 (Days 29–35)** | 130 – 155 g | ~2800 – 2900 g |
| **Week 6 (Days 36–42)** | 160 – 180 g | ~3900 – 4200 g |

*Over the full 42-day cycle, one broiler consumes approximately **4.0 – 4.3 kg** of quality balanced feed.*

---

## 4. Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)
Feed Conversion Ratio is the premier benchmark of farm profitability:
$$\text{FCR} = \frac{\text{Total Feed Consumed (kg)}}{\text{Total Live Body Weight (kg)}}$$
* **Excellent FCR:** 1.6 – 1.7 (1.6–1.7 kg feed required per 1 kg live weight gain).
* **Average FCR:** 1.75 – 1.85.
* **Loss-making FCR:** > 2.0 (feed wasted due to disease, substandard nutrition, or spillage).

---

## 5. Feeder Pan Height Management
* **Feeder Height:** The lip of the feeder pan or trough must align exactly with the bird's **back/crop level**. Low feeders permit birds to flick feed with beaks and feet, discarding up to 15% directly into the litter!
* **Daily Clean-Out:** Permit birds to clean out feeder pans completely for 30–45 minutes once daily to prevent stale fine dust accumulation and mycotoxin formation.
