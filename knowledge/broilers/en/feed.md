# Broiler Phase Feeding

Broilers possess rapid metabolic growth rates. Feeding is structured into three primary phases:

## Feeding Phases:
1. **Starter (Days 1–14):**
   - Crude protein: 21–23%
   - Form: fine crumbs (1.5–2 mm)
   - Stimulates skeletal framing and visceral organ development.

2. **Grower (Days 15–28):**
   - Crude protein: 19–20%
   - Form: pellets (2.5–3 mm)
   - Rapid accretion of lean muscle mass.

3. **Finisher (Days 29–42):**
   - Crude protein: 17.5–18.5%
   - Form: pellets (3–3.5 mm)
   - Final weight gain prior to processing. Antibiotics are strictly banned during this withdrawal phase!

## Golden Rules:
- Feed must remain available 24/7.
- Clean, cool water must always accompany feed. For every 1 kg of dry feed eaten, broilers consume roughly 1.8–2.0 liters of water.
