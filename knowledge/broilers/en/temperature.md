# Temperature Guidelines for Broilers

Temperature is the decisive factor in chick survival during the initial weeks. A day-old chick has no physiological internal thermoregulation.

## Temperature Schedule by Week:
- **Days 1–3:** 32–33°C (at litter level under the brooder)
- **Days 4–7:** 30–31°C
- **Week 2 (Days 8–14):** 27–29°C
- **Week 3 (Days 15–21):** 24–26°C
- **Week 4 (Days 22–28):** 21–23°C
- **Weeks 5–6 (Days 29–42):** 18–20°C

## Chick Behavior as the Ultimate Gauge:
- **Optimal:** Chicks evenly dispersed across the barn, actively feeding, drinking, and resting comfortably.
- **Cold:** Huddling in tight clusters under heaters, loud distress chirping.
- **Hot:** Moving away towards walls, panting with open beaks, spreading wings.
- **Drafts:** Crowding together in one sector away from cold airflow.
