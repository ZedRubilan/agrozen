# Broiler Vaccination

Timely immunization protects the entire flock against mass mortality.

## Core Vaccination Timeline:
1. **Day 1 (at hatchery):**
   - Marek's Disease
   - Infectious Bronchitis (IB)
2. **Day 7:**
   - Newcastle Disease (ND) + Infectious Bronchitis (drinking water or coarse spray)
3. **Days 12–14:**
   - Gumboro Disease (IBD) — via drinking water
4. **Days 20–22:**
   - Revaccination against Gumboro or Newcastle (based on regional veterinary guidance)

## Water Vaccination Rules:
- Deprive birds of water 2 hours before administration (ensuring entire solution is consumed within 1.5–2 hours).
- Water must be completely chlorine-free! Add skimmed milk powder (2 g dry milk per 1 liter water) to stabilize the live viral vaccine.
- Metal drinkers and buckets are strictly prohibited.
