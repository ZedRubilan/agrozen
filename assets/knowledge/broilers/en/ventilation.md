# Broiler Barn Ventilation

Proper ventilation eliminates carbon dioxide, ammonia, and moisture, supplying the flock with fresh oxygen.

## Primary Requirements:
- First 7 days: Minimum ventilation (0.1–0.15 m/s), eliminating drafts at floor level.
- Weeks 2–4: Moderate ventilation providing steady fresh air exchange.
- Weeks 5–6: Maximum fan output, especially during high ambient summer conditions.
- Maximum allowable ammonia concentration: 15 ppm (if humans perceive ammonia odor, levels are already excessive!).

## Heat Stress Protection:
When temperatures exceed 30°C, employ evaporative cooling pads, fogging misters, or roof cold-water sprinkling.
