# Broiler Vaccination: Schedule and Field Principles

> **IMPORTANT NOTICE:** 
> AgroZen does not prescribe vaccine strains or medication dosages. Vaccination is a state veterinary regulation measure. Finalize your specific farm schedule, strain selection (LaSota, Clone, Winterfield), and local disease status with your certified district veterinarian!

---

## 1. Why Is Vaccination Crucial for Broilers?
Broilers live only 40–45 days, but their immunological system is exceptionally delicate. Avian viral diseases (Newcastle Disease, Gumboro / IBD, Infectious Bronchitis) propagate like wildfire: if Newcastle Disease enters an unimmunized barn, **90–100% of the flock can perish within 48–72 hours**. Viral infections cannot be treated with antibiotics — only prophylactic vaccination prevents catastrophic loss.

---

## 2. Standard Broiler Vaccination Timeline

| Day | Disease | Method | Field Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1** | Marek's Disease, Infectious Bronchitis (IB), Newcastle Disease (ND) | Coarse spray or subcutaneous injection at hatchery | Handled at the hatchery prior to chick dispatch (verify veterinary certificate) |
| **Day 7** | Newcastle Disease (ND) + Infectious Bronchitis (IB) | Eye/nose drops or drinking water | Establishes mucosal respiratory barrier |
| **Days 11 – 14** | Gumboro Disease (IBD — Infectious Bursal Disease) | Drinking water | Protects the Bursa of Fabricius (primary immune organ) |
| **Days 18 – 21** | Newcastle Disease booster / Gumboro revaccination | Drinking water | Secures immune titer through slaughter |

---

## 3. Golden Rules of Drinking Water Vaccination
Over 90% of field vaccine failures stem from killing the live viral antigen before birds consume it:
1. **Cold Chain (+2 to +8°C):** Transport vaccine vials from the pharmacy inside insulated ice coolers. Exposure above +10°C or freezing destroys live viral titers.
2. **Chlorine-Free Water:** Tap water chlorine kills live virus particles within 60 seconds! Use untreated spring water or de-chlorinated water rested 48 hours, fortified with milk powder stabilizer.
3. **Blue Dye Indicator:** Use commercial blue dye stabilizer tablets. 1–2 hours following vaccination, inspect chick beaks: a blue tongue and crop verify genuine vaccine ingestion.
4. **Adequate Drinking Space:** Ensure sufficient drinker access so all birds drink concurrently, preventing dominant chicks from overconsuming while timid birds receive none.

---

## 4. Vaccination Contraindications
* Never vaccinate clinically sick, chilled, or severely stressed birds — the live vaccine will trigger outright disease flare-ups.
* Never administer antibacterial drugs 2 days before and 2 days after live viral vaccines.
* Never prepare vaccine solutions in galvanized or rusted metal containers — use food-grade plastic only.
