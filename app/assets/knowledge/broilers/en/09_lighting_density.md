# Lighting Program and Stocking Density (Ross-308)

## Lighting Schedule and Darkness Rest Periods
A structured lighting program is critical for early crop fill, skeletal leg strength, lower mortality, and optimal Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR):

* **Days 1–3 (Brooding & Placement):** **23 hours light and 1 hour darkness** per day at **30–40 lux** floor intensity so every chick easily finds nipple drinkers and starter crumble.
* **Days 4–6:** **20 hours light and 4 hours darkness** (20–25 lux).
* **Days 7–35 (Skeletal Growth & Conditioning):** **18 hours light and 6 hours continuous darkness** at **10 lux**. Six hours of darkness stimulates melatonin, strengthens leg bones, prevents ascites/sudden death syndrome, and improves FCR.
* **Days 36–42 (Pre-Harvest Finisher):** **20 hours light and 4 hours darkness** at 10 lux.

## Stocking Density (Birds per m²)
Overcrowding causes wet caked litter, ammonia burns, heat stress, and uneven flock weights:

* **Days 1–7 (Brooder Ring):** **30–35 chicks/m²**.
* **Days 8–14:** **24–26 birds/m²**.
* **Days 15–21:** **18–20 birds/m²**.
* **Days 22–28:** **15–16 birds/m²**.
* **Days 29–42 (Harvest Target):** **12–14 birds/m²** (maximum live biomass must stay at or below **33–35 kg/m²**).

## Feeder and Drinker Space Norms
* Provide at least **1 nipple drinker per 10–12 birds**.
* Provide at least **1 round pan feeder per 45–50 birds**.
* Adjust equipment height every 2–3 days: keep feeder pan rims level with the bird's back to eliminate spillage, and raise nipples slightly above eye level so birds drink at a 45–60° upward angle.
