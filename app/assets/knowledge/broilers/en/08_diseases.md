# Disease Signs: When to Immediately Consult a Veterinarian

> **CORE AGROZEN PRINCIPLE:** 
> AgroZen does not replace a veterinary physician. If a flock shows acute infectious symptoms, self-treating with blind antibiotic dosing will decimate the flock and cause grave financial loss. Contact your veterinarian at the first dangerous sign!

---

## 1. Red Flag Emergency Symptoms

If you observe any of the following signs, take immediate action:

### 1. Acute Mortality Spike
* **Normal Range:** Baseline daily mortality should never exceed 0.1–0.15% of the total flock (e.g. 1–2 birds per day out of 1,000).
* **CRITICAL ALERT:** If daily mortality surpasses **1% in 24 hours** (> 10 birds out of 1,000), an acute disease outbreak is actively spreading!

### 2. Severe Respiratory Distress
* Loud rattling, whistling, gurgling, or clicking sounds audible across the entire poultry barn.
* Birds craning necks upward, gasping desperately for breath through open beaks.
* Facial edema, periocular swelling ("owl head"), foamy or purulent ocular discharge.

### 3. Alarm Signs in Droppings
* **Bloody or dark reddish-brown droppings:** Hallmarks of acute coccidiosis.
* **Vivid green, watery frothy droppings:** Highly indicative of Newcastle Disease or Gumboro Disease.
* **Chalky white diarrhea pasting the vent:** Sign of pullorum disease (Salmonella pullorum) in young stock.

### 4. Neurological Dysfunction and Paralysis
* Torticollis / twisted neck (birds twisting head backward or upside down).
* Loss of balance, head tremors, clonic leg convulsions.
* Complete wing and leg paralysis (birds stuck in a split posture).

---

## 2. Mandatory Protocol BEFORE the Veterinarian Arrives
1. **Isolation:** Immediately remove suspect and dead birds from the flock. Seal dead birds in clean airtight bags in a cool location for veterinary necropsy.
2. **Review Environmental Equipment:** Inspect temperature, airflow, and drinker lines. Occasionally "disease outbreaks" stem from sudden drinker line blockages or extreme overheating.
3. **NEVER Administer Blind Antibiotics!** Dosing flocks with broad-spectrum antimicrobials before diagnostic sampling renders veterinary lab cultures sterile, preventing accurate pathogen isolation and sensitivity testing.
4. **Compile Flock Records:**
   * Exact flock age in days;
   * Counts of sick and dead birds over the preceding 3 days;
   * Feed manufacturer, ration phase, and batch lot;
   * Administered vaccine brand names, dates, and serial batch numbers;
   * Water supply source (piped municipal, deep borehole, canal).

---

## 3. Biosecurity Rules During Suspected Outbreaks
* Do not enter neighboring poultry farms and forbid outside visitors from entering your premises.
* Don dedicated protective footwear and overalls used solely inside that specific barn.
* Step thoroughly into disinfection mats placed at entryways.
* Dispose of mortalities strictly according to veterinary direction (incineration or deep burial ≥ 1.5 meters layered with bleaching powder). Discarding carcasses into open canals, streets, or trash heaps is illegal and spreads disease.
