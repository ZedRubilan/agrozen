# Water Supply and Drinking System Management

Water is the most crucial yet frequently neglected nutrient for broilers. Birds can survive days without feed, but perish within 24 hours without water. Restricting water immediately stops feed assimilation and halts growth.

---

## 1. Water Demand and the "Water : Feed" Ratio
* Under comfortable temperatures (20–22°C), broilers drink **1.8–2.0 times more** water by weight than dry feed consumed.
* When barn temperatures exceed 30°C, water consumption increases **2.5–3.0 times**.
* A sudden dip in daily water consumption is the earliest clinical herald of impending disease, manifesting 24–48 hours before visible symptoms or mortality.

---

## 2. Drinking Water Quality Standards
Water offered to broilers must meet the same sanitary standards as potable human tap water:
* **Temperature:** Optimally **18–21°C**. Water above 25°C is unpalatable to birds and fosters bacterial growth. Water below 14°C causes gut spasms and chilling.
* **Acidity (pH):** Ideal range is **6.5 – 7.5**. Water exceeding pH 8.0 impairs digestive enzyme efficacy.
* **Microbiological Purity:** Zero coliforms (E. coli) and Salmonella.
* **Biofilm in Water Lines:** Bacterial slime and vitamin residues coat inner PVC pipes. Flush lines under pressure every 2 weeks, and sanitize thoroughly between flocks with stabilized hydrogen peroxide or organic acids.

---

## 3. Selecting and Adjusting Drinker Systems
### Nipple Drinkers (Industry Best Practice)
* **Density:** 1 nipple with drip cup per **10–12 birds**.
* **Height Adjustment:**
  * Days 1–3: Nipple at chick eye level (bird points beak upward at a 45° angle).
  * Day 4 onward: Birds stretch upward at a 65–75° angle while feet remain planted flat on the floor (birds must not stand on tiptoes).
* **Water Pressure:** During Week 1, maintain low pressure (water column 10–15 cm) for effortless triggering. By Week 5, increase column to 30–35 cm.

### Bell / Vacuum Drinkers
* Common in smaller setups: 1 bell drinker per 50 chicks.
* Requires manual scrubbing and refilling at least twice daily.
* Never place bell drinkers directly on loose litter — rest them on plastic platforms to avoid contamination.

---

## 4. Water Administration Protocols for Vaccines and Vitamins
1. **48 Hours Prior to Vaccination:** Terminate all water acidifiers, chlorine, and sanitizers (which destroy live vaccine viruses).
2. **Water Deprivation (Thirsting):** Withdraw water 1.5–2 hours prior to vaccination (in extreme heat, no more than 1 hour).
3. **Stabilizer:** Add non-fat dry skimmed milk powder (2 g per liter) to neutralize residual heavy metals and chlorine traces.
4. The entire vaccine volume must be consumed within **1.5–2 hours**.
