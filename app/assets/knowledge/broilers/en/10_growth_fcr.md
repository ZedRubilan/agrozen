# Broiler Weight Gain, Feed Conversion (FCR) & Efficiency (EPEF)

## Ross-308 Weekly Weight Benchmarks and Target FCR
Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) is total feed consumed divided by total live weight gained. Lower FCR means higher farm profit:

* **Day 7:** Target weight **185 g** (4.4× placement weight), target FCR ~**0.85**.
* **Day 14:** Target weight **510 g**, daily feed **74 g/bird**, target FCR ~**1.10**.
* **Day 21:** Target weight **1050 g**, daily feed **118 g/bird**, target FCR ~**1.28**.
* **Day 28:** Target weight **1720 g**, daily feed **156 g/bird**, target FCR ~**1.43**.
* **Day 35:** Target weight **2420 g**, daily feed **188 g/bird**, target FCR ~**1.56**.
* **Day 42 (Harvest):** Target weight **2950–3080 g**, daily feed **210 g/bird**, cumulative FCR **1.65–1.72**.

## European Production Efficiency Factor (EPEF)
Evaluate overall flock profitability using the EPEF formula:
* **EPEF = (Livability % × Average Live Weight kg) / (Age in Days × FCR) × 100**
* **Above 320:** Elite commercial performance.
* **260–320:** Good flock management.
* **Below 260:** Investigate feed spillage, house temperature swings, or poor pellet durability.

## 5 Golden Rules for Maximum Healthy Broiler Growth
1. **Day-7 Weight Sets the Ceiling:** Every +10g on Day 7 translates to +50–60g additional harvest weight on Day 42.
2. **Water Drives Feed Intake:** Broilers drink **1.8–2.0× more water by weight than feed**. If water lines warm up above 22°C or clog, feed intake drops immediately.
3. **Pellet Physical Quality:** From Day 11 onward, use durable 3.0–3.5 mm pellets with minimal fines (<10% dust); dusty feed degrades FCR by 10–15%.
4. **Weekly Sample Weighing:** Weigh at least 30–50 random birds from 3 barn locations every 7 days to track uniformity.
5. **Pre-Harvest Feed Withdrawal:** Remove feed **8–10 hours before catching** to clear the digestive tract, while keeping **clean drinking water available** until catching begins.
